The Metcalfe's Law Connection

A network of n nodes can make n(n-1)/2 connections. While the number of participants grows linearly (1, 2, 3, 4...), the number of possible interactions grows quadratically (0, 1, 3, 6...). This explains why platforms like telephone networks, languages, and social media tend toward monopoly: the larger network is mathematically more valuable to every participant.
